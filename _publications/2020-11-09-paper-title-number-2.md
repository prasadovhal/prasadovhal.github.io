The standard Black Hole algorithm uses a single attractor to guide the search, which can limit diversity and cause premature convergence. This paper introduces Twin and Multiple Black Hole variants that maintain several competing attractors simultaneously, improving exploration of the feature space. Paired with an SVM classifier, the new variants outperform the original algorithm on feature subset quality and classification accuracy.
