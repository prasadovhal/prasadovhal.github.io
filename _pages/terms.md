## Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors engage with it. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data — including pages visited, time on site, and general geographic location — via cookies, without identifying individual visitors.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking via the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
